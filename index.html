How to make Phonk Potstickers.

Recipe time: 670 seconds

Ingredients needed

  • Frozen potstickers
  • Peanut oil
  • Potsticker sauce
  • Phonk 💀

Instructions

  1. Add 1 tablespoon of oil to a nonstick pan and coat evenly.
  2. Place frozen potstickers flat, bottom-side down, leaving space between each.
  3. Add water and turn on Medium heat. (For 5 pieces, 1/2 cup. For 10 pieces, 2/3 cup. For 15 pieces, 1 cup)
  4. Bring to boil. Cover and simmer on Medium heat for 8 minutes.
  5. Uncover. Do not move potstickers. Continue cooking until water evaporates and bottoms of potstickers are lightly browned and...
  6. ADD PHONK
